What items are eligible for return?

All tableware, dinner sets, carafes, apparel, and lifestyle accessories are eligible for returns within 7 calendar days of receipt. Items must be unwashed, unbroken, and returned with original protective box cushions.

What if a piece arrives damaged during transit?

While our honeycomb packaging has a 99.7% safe arrival record, rough transit can rarely cause breakage. If an item arrives damaged, simply snap a photo and email concierge@availsmart.com or WhatsApp our desk. We dispatch an instant replacement the same day—no questions asked.
