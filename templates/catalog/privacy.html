Transparency & Ethics

Privacy Policy & Studio Ethics

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • AvailSmart India Private Limited

1. Our Ethical Commitment

AvailSmart respects your personal privacy as fiercely as we respect the artisan traditions of our craftspeople. We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers or advertisement networks.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly essential to curate, fulfill, and deliver your orders:

  • Contact identifiers (Name, Email address, Phone number for courier dispatch).
  • Delivery coordinates (Shipping address, postal pincode, state).
  • Order history and studio preferences (Saved wishlist pieces, cart items).

3. Payment & Data Security

We do not store your raw credit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers. All transaction flows are secured using end-to-end 256-bit SSL encryption, tokenized directly with PCI-DSS compliant financial gateways.

4. Communications & Newsletter

You will only receive editorial stories, drop announcements, or private sale invitations if you have explicitly joined the AvailSmart Private Circle. You can unsubscribe with 1-click anytime via the footer of our emails.

5. Data Rectification & Deletion

You hold the fundamental right to inspect, edit, or delete your account records. To request account deletion or address purging, contact our privacy officer at privacy@availsmart.com.
