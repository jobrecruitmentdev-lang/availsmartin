✦ Studio Manifesto

Shaped by Hand. Made for Slow Mornings & Quiet Living.

AvailSmart was founded on a simple dissatisfaction with fast-disposable consumer plastic. We believe everyday objects should carry permanence, texture, and quiet calm.

01

High-Fire Kiln Vitrification

Our tableware is fired at 1,220°C in small-batch kilns. This fuses the clay into a non-porous, stone-hard ceramic that never absorbs liquids or stains.

100% Dishwasher & Microwave Safe
02

Mineral Sourced Glazes

We avoid synthetic chemical coatings. Every shade—from deep slate forest to creamy warm oat—is derived from natural feldspar and iron mineral glazes.

Zero Lead • Zero Cadmium
03

Compostable Protective Packaging

No plastic bubble wrap. Every ceramic piece is nestled in recycled honeycomb paper cushioning and unbleached Kraft boxes that break down organically.

100% Recyclable • Plastic-Free
Our Philosophy

An antidote to the ephemeral.

When you hold an AvailSmart mug or pour from our ceramic carafes, you feel the weight of the maker’s hand. We work directly with master potters in Khurja, Jaipur, and regional artisan cooperatives across India.

By removing intermediate middlemen, we ensure fair livable wages for artisans while offering museum-grade homeware directly to conscious homes.
