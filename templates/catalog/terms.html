1. The Nature of Handmade Stoneware

Every ceramic piece on AvailSmart is molded and glazed by real human hands. Slight organic variations in glaze dripping, matte speckling, and millimeters of diameter are celebrations of natural stoneware, not manufacturer defects. No two studio pieces are identical.

2. Pricing, Invoicing & Taxes

All prices listed across our catalog are expressed in Indian Rupees (INR) and are inclusive of Goods & Services Tax (GST). A formal printable GST Tax Invoice is generated automatically upon successful order confirmation.

3. Shipping & Transit

AvailSmart partners with top national courier services (Bluedart, Delhivery, Express Ecom) to fulfill deliveries safely. While dispatch occurs within 24 to 48 business hours, estimated transit timelines may fluctuate during extreme weather or national holidays.

4. 7-Day In-Home Trial & Cancellations

Clients enjoy a full 7-day in-home trial period. Unwashed, undamaged items are eligible for doorstep return. Orders may be cancelled by the customer prior to fulfillment directly from their account dashboard.
